Interpretation

Metabolic disorders → expected pCO₂

Formulas
  • Winter's formula (metabolic acidosis): expected pCO₂ (mmHg) = 1.5 × HCO₃⁻ + 8 ± 2. In kPa: (1.5 × HCO₃⁻ + 8) ÷ 7.5 ± 0.27.
  • Quick check (metabolic acidosis): expected pCO₂ (mmHg) ≈ last two digits of pH (e.g. pH 7.25 → ~25 mmHg), valid for pH 7.10–7.60; or ≈ HCO₃⁻ + 15.
  • Metabolic alkalosis: pCO₂ rises 0.7 mmHg per 1 mmol/L rise in HCO₃⁻ → expected = 40 + 0.7 × (HCO₃⁻ − 24) ± 5; alternative: 0.7 × HCO₃⁻ + 21 ± 2. Respiratory compensation rarely takes pCO₂ above ~7.3–8 kPa (55–60 mmHg).
  • Measured pCO₂ higher than expected → co-existing respiratory acidosis. Lower → co-existing respiratory alkalosis.
